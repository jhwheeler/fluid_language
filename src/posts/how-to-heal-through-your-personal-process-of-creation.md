The question of who I am is constantly arising. What is the truth of my being, the essence of my experience, and the foundation of my perception? All of this seems so abstract and yet so tangible, a paradox of becoming and being, the intertwining of spacetime and consciousness.

Action defines me, and awareness undefines me. Limitlessness at the core, infinitely intricate delineations expanding out of that nucleus, shaping the fabric of all.

The key is to expand from the core while always staying rooted in it. It needs to truly be the core itself that is expanding, rather than unrelated new structures interceding and masking the core. That is the nature of inauthenticity: the core, rather than expanding its own essence, is constantly being layered over, covered, masked with tangentially  connected ideas, plans, and conclusions. If we stay this way, the core never truly is, since the core's nature is to expand. If it does nnot expand, it is not fully itself.

So remember: whatever you're feeeling right now, that is the core of this moment. What you experience on all levels -- physically, emotionally, mentally, spiritually -- all of this is the core of the now. 

Start from now. Rather than going back and trying to grasp that which was 1 minute ago -- maintaining something from the past as an afterthought -- focus on starting from here and now. So ask yourself, what is arising now? Take that and go into it. Live it. Live through it. Live as it. Live as this moment now. As this now expands and evolves, the key is to maintain the entire thread from that first full dropping into the moment. Stay focused, stay aware -- don't forget where you are coming from -- and always continue staying with what arises within and without yourself. 

See how long you can keep this up. Allow all new thoughts as continuations of the last experience; all the while, you are maintaining the presence of the now and the continuity of this thread of awareness.

In this way, authenticity is born. This is the true becoming of the authentic self -- pure awareness embodied fully and effortlessly in the presence of what's arising here and now. All actions arising are the outpouring of that initial core...ever-expanding all the time in every which way, but always maintaining that continuity of awareness...

Inevitabily, this will of course dissolve. You will get distracted and be taken away from the initial moment of stepping into presence. That's fine. Bring it back to this moment and keep going.

Remember...and allow.
